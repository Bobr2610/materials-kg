# Pilot Notes: CuCrZr And 316L Process Follow-Up

The Thermal Processing Group reviewed LPBF CuCrZr coupons produced on the Renishaw AM500Q. The as-built coupons showed electrical conductivity near 42 %IACS. After solution treatment, conductivity increased to about 58 %IACS. The team noted that the next missing step is an aged CuCrZr condition, because the current data does not yet prove the target conductivity window after full heat treatment.

For 316L stainless steel, HIP improved relative density to 99.8 percent. The same review found that HIP alone did not reduce surface roughness enough for final-use parts. The group recommended a separate surface finishing experiment before drawing conclusions about fatigue-sensitive applications.

Decision notes:

- CuCrZr aging data is a priority gap for conductivity coverage.
- 316L HIP results should be linked to density improvement, not to surface finish readiness.
- Renishaw AM500Q builds should keep source metadata with machine, layer thickness, and atmosphere when available.
